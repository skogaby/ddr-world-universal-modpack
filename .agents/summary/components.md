## src/lib.rs — Entry Point

`DllMain` (spawns `init()` on a thread), the full numbered init sequence, mod construction/registration in registration order, and the boot splash (title/author/menu-hint text widgets, plus a red "REBOOT THE GAME" warning when texture atlases were rebuilt this boot; all destroyed after 10 s).

### song_rate/ (largest subsystem)

Per-player rate-adjusted playback, streamed with no disk cache. Submodules: `binding` (virtual-bank binding + registry + preview slot), `generator` (producer thread, WSOLA/resample seam), `io_callback_hook` + `wavebank_hook` (the four detours, pair-or-neither), `transaction` (exactly-once commit: score→movie→snapshot→clock), `lifecycle` (pure eligibility/phase machine), `runtime` (scene callback, desired-value atomics, maintenance drain), `clock_patch` (Q31 gameplay-clock byte patch), `preview` (song-select preview rate + live-edit restart), `real_speed` (rate-aware Real Speed recompute), `tick_domain` (content↔wall algebra for assist tick), `selected_song` (seqlock publication of the highlighted song), `xact_runtime` (slots/frames/queues). Eleven host-test files.
