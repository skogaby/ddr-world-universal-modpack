# Rough Idea: Song Playback Speed

Add a feature that lets a player alter the playback speed of in-game songs
during gameplay.

Use `docs/song_playback_speed.md` as preliminary feasibility research and a
starting point for recommendations, not as an already-approved requirements or
design document.

The feature must fit the existing Rust hook DLL architecture, preserve game
stability and chart/audio synchronization, and maintain the repository's
score-integrity policy for assisted play.
