# Rough Idea: 2-Player BPL Mode

New top-level mod, "2-player BPL Mode". When enabled, if the gameplay is a 2-player
versus session, force-enable the in-game versus UI experience that is normally
reserved for in-shop matching mode (the BPL "battle" HUD).

In-shop matching allows 4-player competition between 2 linked cabinets, but the
experience adds a lot to gameplay in terms of letting spectators easily track the
current leader during gameplay (per-player score boards, score-ratio bars, live
1st/2nd rank badges, score-margin readout).

Starting point: `docs/in_shop_battle_local_versus_research.md` (display-side RE +
re-hosting plan) and the companion `docs/bpl_battle_mode_research.md` (network stack).

Requested process: a light PDD pass.
