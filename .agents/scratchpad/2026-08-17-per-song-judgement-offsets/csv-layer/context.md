## Ambiguities → resolutions (auto mode)
- Header detection: treat the first line as a header iff its first cell is
  literally `code` (case-insensitive) — codes are lowercase basenames but a
  song named "code" is conceivable; accept the negligible risk and document.
- `ParseStats` offending line numbers: cap at 8 retained (enough for a WARN).
- Clamp applies to in-range-parseable integers only; a non-integer cell makes
  the LINE skip (per task: "unparseable lines skipped") — no partial-row
  salvage. Rationale: machine-managed file, partial salvage complicates the
  contract for no benefit.
- Line with more than 3 cells: unparseable (skip + stats). Fewer than 3
  cells: missing cells = blank (tolerates hand-trimmed rows).
