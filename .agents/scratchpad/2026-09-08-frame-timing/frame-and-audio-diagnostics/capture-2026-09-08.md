## Interpretation and Methods

- Frame Hz uses differences of the actual frame sequence divided by QPC elapsed
  time, not retained row count. The CSV is decimated. Each retained post-anchor
  frame reports one queued job executed and one job remaining; no observed queue
  growth or continuation multiplication. This validates cadence on this run,
  not a before/after CPU saving or physical input-loss fix.
- Gap maxima come from the recorder's all-observation accumulator. They are
  intervals between judge-update observations, NOT human hits or CSV rows. The
  95.828 ms record occurs at chart 4.194 seconds (CSV 12587): stored prior count
  4098, current 4194, consistent with a real update gap. Attempt 3 also has an
  84.509 ms gap near chart 0.405 seconds (CSV 12379). Cause is unassigned.
- Frame max_gap_qpc is cumulative from scene entry and includes pre-song loading.
  Its 2.408-second attempt-1 value is BEFORE anchoring, not a mid-song freeze.
  No full-frame p99 is inferred from decimated or conditionally retained rows.
- Fit a line to judge_mc versus QPC milliseconds within each valid constant
  segment, chart time >=3 seconds. Slopes are 1.0000111-1.0000120, equivalent to
  0.667-0.718 ms/minute, or about 1.2 ms over a complete part play. Detrended
  retained-sample residuals are approximately millisecond-scale.
- Across the common chart interval 3-50 seconds, median
  judge_mc - elapsed_since_start_request + sound_ms - option_ms is
  +0.653, +0.158, -0.390, +0.341, -0.319 ms: about 1.043 ms spread, not tens of ms.
- QPC minus the absolute game-clock anchor changes about 23.62 ms across the
  session. Do NOT call this a carried-over 23.62 ms song offset: each song takes
  a fresh anchor from the same game clock, cancelling its accumulated epoch
  difference. Per-song elapsed rate and unmeasured audio-start phase are the
  relevant remaining questions.
- There is no actual audio presentation channel or pre-fix/no-diagnostic control.
  The small rate difference is game-clock versus QPC on Wine, not demonstrated
  audio drift or the original Win7 problem. Do not apply a ppm correction from
  these observations alone.
