## Facts from code reading
- `capture::create_hook` gates its ENTIRE body on
  `overlay_element_styling::is_enabled()` (MOD_ENABLED) — with styling
  config-disabled the registry tracks nothing. Shared install therefore
  needs a widened TRACKING gate (`styling enabled OR shared consumer`),
  while the styling APPLY inside `bind_and_apply` stays value-driven (the
  scale/opacity atomics are identity-seeded and only move when styling's
  rows exist ⇒ identity no-op applies are safe when styling is off).
- `capture::remove()` is called by styling's enable ROLLBACK (color hook
  failure) — must become a no-op while a shared consumer holds the capture.
- `install_create`/`install_set_position` go through
  `core::hooks::install_enabled(&mut STATIC, ...)` — need an
  already-installed ⇒ true guard for idempotency (both callers).
- `bm2d_api::mc_op` is numeric-only (3rd arg widened to u64 at the FFI) —
  add `mc_op_str` passing a `&CStr` pointer as the u64 (op 0xF09 =
  goto-frame-label-by-string, precedent: FC splash RE §5).
- MC id lives at wrapper+0x110 (pool layout, §5 RE + bm2d docs);
  layer id at +0x08. Validate `mc_id >= 1` before use.
- Registry access is game-thread-only; the judge tap runs inside
  judge_submit (game thread) — safe.
