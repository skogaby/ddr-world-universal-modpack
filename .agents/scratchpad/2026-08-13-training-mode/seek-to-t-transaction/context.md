## Domain-math decision (rate composition — recorded reasoning)
The Step-1 mapping is in SERVED-STREAM blocks (wall domain at rate; identity ⇒
source grid — the design's B(T) letter). The exact chain at any rate:
- `wall_t = content_to_wall(t_ms)` (identity ⇒ t_ms),
- `quantize_seek(wall_t, spb, sample_rate, stream_blocks)` → `{shift_blocks, W_q}`
  (task-02's pure fn, fed wall-domain input — the served grid),
- `t_q = wall_to_content(W_q)` (identity ⇒ W_q) — the content playhead for the
  rebuild/notify, sub-ms round-trip slop inherent to integer ms domains,
- anchor = `seek::anchor_tick(now, delay_wall_q, t_q, snapshot)` =
  `now + delay − wall(t_q)` (the design §4.4 formula composed with the shipped
  future-dating term),
- `lead_blocks` = `quantize_seek(delay_ms, …)` on the same served grid, and the
  anchor's delay term is the block-quantized lead's ms — audio silence and clock
  lead stay the same length.
Delayed seeks do NOT use the shipped countdown driver: the mapping's lead IS the
silent approach (replay immediately; single prepared→anchor adjacency), unlike
t=0 where no lead mechanism exists. This is req 6's composition.

## Interpretation & assumptions (auto mode)
- `SEEK_END_MARGIN_MS = 1000` (design names a margin, no value; 1 s keeps T_q
  a full cascade-check period below the raw threshold).
- Versus: clamp against the MIN of both sides' `chart_end_raw`; any actor
  missing its CMA child ⇒ `Refused`.
- `quantize to blocks == 0` allowed (a near-0 seek is a valid mapping {0, lead}).
- Bounded WARNs: per-reason one-shot latches (once per boot).
- `Keep` policy: refused at request time in v1 (reserved for v2 FF/RW).
