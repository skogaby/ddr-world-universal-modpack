## Interpretation & assumptions (auto mode)
- Quantization: `blocks = floor(t·rate / (spb·1000))` (frame-domain floor), clamped to
  `[0, max_blocks]` (max from the caller's clamp inputs — audio length); `t_q_ms =
  round_half_up(blocks·spb·1000, rate)` (the established half-up convention for the grid
  point's ms value). Negative t clamps to 0. Zero spb/rate ⇒ None (invalid inputs).
- The rebuild-expectation model indexes records by non-control note order (the engine
  appends one record per kind ≥ 0 note); expectations carry the note index for pairing.
- Freeze head↔end pairing: a head is a kind-0 note with any duration > 0; its end is the
  FIRST following kind-2 note whose panel participation mask (nonzero-ness of the 8 flags)
  equals the head's. The kind-2 back-patch targets the LATEST earlier freeze-participant
  record with a matching mask (mirrors the engine's backward walk).
- Neutralization writes are `(byte_offset_from_records_base, i32 value)` pairs: head
  hold-progress 8 writes (all 8 panels — non-participants carry duration 0, matching the
  engine's "full per-panel durations" copy), end grade 6 + judgedAt.
