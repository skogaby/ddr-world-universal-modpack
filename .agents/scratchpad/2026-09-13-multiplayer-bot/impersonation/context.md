## Offsets (design §5.1, build-invariant per §2.3)
- GameWork: `+0x0` versus (i32, WRITE), `+0x4` style, course word at `course_field_offset()`
  (u64), event via `stage_records::event_mode()`.
- PlayerWork: `+0x4` entered (u8, WRITE), `+0xC..+0x15` name (9 bytes, WRITE), `+0x50/+0x54/+0x5C`
  style/mcode/difficulty (i32, MIRROR), Option at `+player_option_offset()`.
- Option: copy `+0x08..=0x6C` (0x68 bytes), force `+0x18 = 0`. Never `+0x00`.
- Record: `+0x00` mcode (compare), `+0x04` diff / `+0x08` style (MIRROR).
